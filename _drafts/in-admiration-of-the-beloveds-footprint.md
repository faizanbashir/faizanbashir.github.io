One blessed day luck smiled upon me for I saw the footprint of the beloved,
a surreal joy took shape in the sanctity of my heart,
for a long time, I had been bewailing the absence of that majestic presence,
lost in admiration I sat with legs crossed like a child perplexed in thought,
although blossoms were sprouting in the garden of my heart,
I sat awestruck by that sight, the print of the holy foot had caused such an inexplicable joy,
Imagine, imagine for the sake of life itself what joy would arise from the sight of the beloved's face,
the glitter of all trade and worldliness soon turned sour in the presence of that majesty,
although the beloved used to admonish his companions(r.a) to acquire worldly skills,
skills in archery and excellence in knowledge,
all of this was meant by way of informing his eternal subjects,
to open the gate of hereafter by the key of good worldly deeds,
that the strong believer is the beloved of God,
for his strength and fortunes lay as an endowment for the glory of the beloved's faith,
a hundred times the pious is sacrificing his head in rapture for the love of the beloved,
knowledge, glorious enterprise, spiritual seclusion,
the believer undertakes myriad pains for that sake of a single glimpse of the face that shone brighter than the moon,
for the sake of bringing a smile to that illustrious face, entire lives were devoted,
O my believing brother you and I were born to bring glory to the Prophets(s.a.w) faith,
many have passed this way before us whose sweat and blood you behold,
the world sings the praises of Muslims even Europe is indebted to them for enlightenment and the departure of the dark ages,
our forbearers all lovers of the beloved, for him they strove for him they gained knowledge,
they deemed the knowledge greatest which came from the "mercy unto the worlds",
Faizan in all existence would desire to do nothing other than to gaze at the world illuminating face of the beloved,
but for the beloveds sake O brother, we must toil,
in knowledge, in deeds, in kindness, even brave enterprise do whatever you must for the love of the beloved,
For thus spoke Iqbal the wise "ki Muhammad se wafa tune to hum tere hai, ye jahan cheez hai kya loh-o-qalam tere hai".
